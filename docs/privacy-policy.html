SortVerse 3D Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

SortVerse 3D is designed to work offline. The game does not require registration, an account or an internet connection. The app developer does not operate a game backend or analytics service and does not collect or transmit your game progress, profile name or photos to the developer.

Information stored on your device

Game levels, stars, local rankings, achievements, coin balances, daily challenge history, settings and optional profile information are stored locally on your device. Android may also store a local copy of progress using Capacitor Preferences. This is not cloud synchronization. Uninstalling or clearing app data may erase this information.

Camera and photos

Taking a selfie or selecting a photo is optional. Camera and photo permissions are only used when you explicitly open those features. Selected images are cropped and stored locally for your avatar and full-size preview. They are not automatically uploaded to the game developer. The native system camera/photo picker and operating system may have their own privacy behavior.

Sharing and backups

When you use the share button, a performance receipt image is handed to the app you select via the device share sheet. That third-party app's privacy practices apply. You can export a progress code and copy or share it manually. This code can contain your profile picture and other stored data; treat it as private. Importing a code replaces existing SortVerse progress after confirmation.

Audio and device features

Optional sounds, music, vibration and device text-to-speech are controlled by Settings. Text-to-speech is provided by the device's voice engine. Some voice engines may have their own online processing or policies; you can turn sound effects off.

Advertising, purchases and children

The game does not display ads, track players or sell user data. No in-app purchase system is included. Coins and diamonds are earned within the game. The publisher declares the actual intended audience in Google Play's target-audience settings.

Data deletion

Choose Profile > Reset progress to remove saved game data, or clear the app's storage/uninstall it. Saved backup codes you personally copied elsewhere are not deleted by this action.
