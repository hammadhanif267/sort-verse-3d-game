# Gameplay HUD and mobile readability update

- Mission heading, level title and objective now have dedicated line spacing in the header.
- The board has a reserved vertical area; status messages and booster buttons are in a separate lower toolbar.
- On smaller screens the tube geometry scales down to leave room for controls.
- Moves, Undo, Hint and conditional Rescue remain visible with larger touch targets.
- The redundant floating Reset button is removed. Restart remains in Pause and now asks for confirmation.
- Level Selection now scrolls vertically with two-column, larger cards and readable mission names.
- Bottom navigation touch targets and small labels on menu screens are enlarged.

All gameplay mechanics and saved progress formats are unchanged. Screen geometry must be verified on real phones, including a short landscape-free Android handset and a device with a gesture bar or notch.
